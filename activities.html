Easy ideas for any day

Forest bathing walk

Walk slowly for 30 minutes at Riverbend and notice five sounds, five smells and five colors.

Sunset by the water

Grab a bench on the Lakeview pier and watch the light change. Free, quiet and surprisingly relaxing.

Plan your visit

Pick a trail, check the weather and tell a friend where you are going. Most park paths have cell coverage, but the Old Quarry lookout can be patchy.
