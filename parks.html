Parks & green spaces

Four favorites to start with. Each one has something different to offer.

Find your park

Lakeview Commons

WaterFamilyAccessible

A flat 2 km path circles the lake, so strollers and wheelchairs roll easily. Paddle boats are available on weekends, and the picnic shelters can be booked for birthdays.

Facilities: restrooms, drinking fountains, playground, bike racks.

Maple Hill Gardens

PicnicFlowersPets welcome

Bring a blanket and claim a spot on the big lawn. The garden path blooms with tulips in spring and glows with maple color in autumn. Dogs on leash are welcome.

Facilities: restrooms, picnic tables, dog water station, small cafe kiosk.

Old Quarry Trail

HikingModerateSunrise

A 6 km loop with a few steady climbs and a lookout point halfway round. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water, as there are no fountains on the trail.

Facilities: parking lot, trail map board, benches at the lookout.

Riverbend Park

ShadeRunningBikes

A shaded riverside path that runs 8 km from downtown to the old mill. It is popular with joggers and cyclists, so keep to the right and ring your bell when passing.

Facilities: restrooms at each end, bike repair stand, benches every 500 m.

Park visit tips

What should I bring?

Water, sun protection, a small bag for litter and comfortable shoes. A light jacket helps in the early morning.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, on a leash no longer than two meters. Please clean up after your pet.

Can I book a picnic shelter?

Shelters at Lakeview Commons and Maple Hill Gardens can be booked. Send us a message and choose "Picnic shelter booking".
