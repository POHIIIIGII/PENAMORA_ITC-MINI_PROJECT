Why spend an afternoon outside?

A short walk under trees lowers stress, gets you moving and gives you a reason to put your phone away. Here is how Greenway City makes it easy.

  • 12 public parks
  • 48 km of marked trails
  • 6 free playgrounds
  • Daily open 6 am to 9 pm
